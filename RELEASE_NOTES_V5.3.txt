My Finance Tracker - Version 5.3

Payment method details
- One reusable method-specific form is used by payment requests, debt payments, and EMI/Loan payment editing.
- Mobile banking supports bKash, Nagad, Rocket, Upay, or another provider, with mobile/account number, holder, and transaction ID.
- Bank transfer and cheque use a searchable offline Bangladeshi bank picker plus an Other bank option.
- Bank details support account holder, account number, branch, routing number, and reference.
- Salary deduction, card, cash, cheque, and other methods display and validate only relevant fields.
- PINs, passwords, CVVs, and full card numbers are never requested.

Debt navigation
- Money I Owe and Money Owed to Me are separate pages with separate totals, lists, search, sorting, and Add actions.
- New records inherit a locked direction from the selected page, preventing accidental direction selection.

Payment requests
- Save PDF, Share, Edit, and Cancel use one compact action row.
- Structured method details appear in generated payment-request PDFs.

Reports
- Search and filter by report type, period, custom date range, status, and sorting.
- Results show matching counts, filtered totals, and collapsible EMI, Loan, debt-direction, and Expense sections.
- Export a concise Summary PDF, complete Detailed PDF, or filtered Excel report.

My Finance Tracker - Version 5.2

Debt corrections
- Blocks payments when a debt is completed.
- Rechecks the latest remaining balance when saving, preventing duplicate-tap and stale-screen overpayments.
- Prevents edited debt payments from making the paid total exceed the original debt.

Payment requests
- New requests cannot exceed the unreserved receivable balance.
- Received payments can be linked to an open request.
- A single open request is selected automatically when recording received money.
- Request states: UNPAID, PARTIALLY PAID, PAID and CANCELLED.
- Open requests can be edited, but not below money already received.
- Cancellation requires a warning and remains permanently visible as a read-only record.
- Regenerated PDFs show the latest amount received and request status.

Plans and payment history
- All active, completed and archived records have a read-only Plan Information page.
- Payment-history actions use separate compact rows so Save PDF and Share remain visible.
- Completed debts no longer show a usable payment-entry form.

Receipt Profile and signature
- Validates owner name, phone number, email and address.
- Supports an optional PNG, JPEG or WebP signature image.
- Signature is stored in encrypted app data and encrypted backups.
- Signature appears on generated payment receipt and payment-request PDFs.

Additional validation
- Added practical length and range validation across EMI, Loan, Debt, Expense, Payment and Payment Request forms.

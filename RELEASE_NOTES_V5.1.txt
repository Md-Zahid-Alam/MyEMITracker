My Finance Tracker - Version 5.1

User experience
- EMI, Loan, and Debt now open from separate payment pages.
- Each plan has a focused overview with separate payment, history, documents, and financing-information screens.
- Completed and archived plans remain view-only until reopened or restored.

Payments and expenses
- Debt payments and received amounts support a selected past transaction date.
- Expense documents can be opened directly from the expense card.
- Payment requests now use the clearer label "Payment requested from".

Documents and reports
- PDF receipts, payment requests, and reports use a branded teal header, structured information rows, wrapped text, ownership footer, and page numbers.

Validation
- Added limits and checks for names, amounts, interest, installment counts, future dates, payment methods, bank/provider information, references, and notes.

Build
- Version name: 5.1
- Version code: 15
- GitHub Actions produces a signed release APK and Play Store AAB.
